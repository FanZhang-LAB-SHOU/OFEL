Junyi Zhang

张峻溢

PhD Student, Year 1

Junyi Zhang is a PhD student at Shanghai Ocean University working in fisheries ecology and fisheries oceanography. His research examines how climate change affects habitat suitability and the spatial and temporal distribution of fishery resources. He also studies fish community responses to environmental change and interactions among species, alongside food-web structure and trophic relationships in marine ecosystems.

Over the past three years, he has published two first-authored papers in SCI-indexed journals and two first-authored papers in journals indexed by the Chinese Science Citation Database (CSCD).
