Xiaoci Wu

武孝慈

PhD Student, Year 2

Xiaoci Wu is a PhD student at Shanghai Ocean University working in fisheries oceanography and stock assessment. His research examines how climate change and mesoscale ocean processes shape cephalopod distributions and population dynamics.

He has contributed to projects supported by the Shanghai Natural Science Foundation and open research funds from key laboratories in offshore fisheries development and satellite ocean environment dynamics. His work includes processing fisheries and ocean environmental data, analysing the spatial and temporal patterns of fishing grounds, and building habitat suitability models.

He also participated in a comprehensive high-seas fisheries survey in the western and central Pacific, spending about six weeks at sea. Over the past three years, he has published four first-authored papers in SCI-indexed journals.
