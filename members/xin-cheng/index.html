Xin Cheng

程心

Postdoctoral Researcher

cx_shhy@163.comGitHub (opens in a new tab)

Xin Cheng is a postdoctoral researcher at the Oceanic Fisheries Ecosystem Laboratory, Shanghai Ocean University. Her research uses artificial intelligence and vessel-tracking data to study fishing-vessel behaviour and tuna fleet dynamics. She develops deep learning methods for vessel classification and examines how fishing activity varies across regions and over time. Her recent work also assesses the quality of Automatic Identification System (AIS) data to support fisheries monitoring and management.
