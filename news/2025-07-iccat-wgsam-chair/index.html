Fan Zhang appointed Chair of ICCAT's Working Group on Stock Assessment Methods

OFEL's principal investigator is the first scientist from China to chair an ICCAT working group since the country joined the Commission in 1996.

Honors & appointments

Lab members: Fan Zhang

The International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas (ICCAT) has appointed Professor Fan Zhang, principal investigator of OFEL, as Chair of its Working Group on Stock Assessment Methods (WGSAM). The group sits under ICCAT's Standing Committee on Research and Statistics (SCRS). Fan Zhang is the first scientist from China to chair an ICCAT working group since China joined the Commission in 1996.

About ICCAT and WGSAM

ICCAT is the intergovernmental organisation responsible for the conservation and management of tunas and tuna-like species in the Atlantic Ocean and adjacent seas, with its Secretariat in Madrid. The SCRS is its scientific advisory body, and it and its working groups meet several times each year to discuss stock assessments, management measures and science-based decision-making. WGSAM leads the Commission's work on stock assessment methods, coordinating approaches internationally and advancing new techniques.

A role that matches the lab's research

Fan Zhang is China's chief scientist for ICCAT and has long worked on stock assessment models and management strategies. The appointment connects closely with OFEL's research on assessment methods, from how uncertainty is specified in tuna assessments to new models for species with unusual life histories, and with the lab's wider involvement in regional fisheries management organisations.
