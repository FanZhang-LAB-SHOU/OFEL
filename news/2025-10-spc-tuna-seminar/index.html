From the Pacific to Shanghai: using science to support sustainable tuna fisheries

A joint SPC–Shanghai Ocean University seminar featuring Graham Pilling, Robert Scott, Joe Scutt Phillips, and Nan Yao.

Academic exchange

The Pacific Community (SPC) and Shanghai Ocean University announced a joint seminar, “From the Pacific to Shanghai: Using Science to Support Sustainable Tuna Fisheries.” The programme addressed how scientific approaches can support sustainable tuna fisheries in the Pacific region and worldwide.

Seminar details

Date
Saturday, 11 October 2025
Time
10:00–11:30 (Shanghai time)
Venue
Room 319, College of Marine Living Resource Sciences and Management, Shanghai Ocean University
Co-hosts
Pacific Community (SPC) and Shanghai Ocean University

Speakers

Graham Pilling, Robert Scott, Joe Scutt Phillips, and Nan Yao were the invited speakers from SPC. The seminar was open to faculty and students.
